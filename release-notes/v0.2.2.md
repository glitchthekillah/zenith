# Stable loading, faster search and reliable server switching

- Home updates counts and actions without recreating the greeting, restarting its entrance animation or repeatedly resetting the hero.
- Search shows each server's results as they arrive. Movie and show results do not wait for episode searches, reading servers or request providers.
- Server-copy options verify matching TMDB, IMDb or TVDB identifiers before offering another copy. Unrelated search results are rejected.
- Cached item IDs and playback actions are scoped to their source server, preventing another server's item with the same ID from replacing the selected movie.
- Playback options are scoped to the selected server's copy.

Validated with JavaScript regressions and browser checks covering staggered loading, a stalled secondary server, unchanged greeting/search nodes, same-title server switching and successful playback from the selected server.
