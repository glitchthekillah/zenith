## Zenith 0.2.0

**Smaller and faster.** Zenith now runs on Tauri instead of Electron. The download is a fraction of the size, and the app uses much less memory.

### New
- Episode pages show a wide picture of the episode instead of a tall poster.
- Pictures show a soft blurred preview while they load.
- Switching seasons on a show keeps your place on the page.

### Fixed
- Some episodes started from the beginning while the progress bar showed the middle. They now start where you left off.

### Updating from 0.1.x
This version can't install itself over the old one. Download `Zenith_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it, then uninstall the old Zenith. Your servers, sign-ins and settings move over by themselves on first launch.

From now on, updates install by themselves again.
